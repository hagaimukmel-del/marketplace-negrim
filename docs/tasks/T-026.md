## Context
Owner 2026-10-08: a supplier must state where it delivers. Today suppliers have no regions; a
carpenter in Eilat can order from a supplier that never reaches there. `lib/regions.ts` already
holds the seven regions and `regionsForCity()`, used by carpenter registration and the Metzion.

## Done when
- Delivery regions (and an optional delivery fee / free-delivery threshold) are a required field
  in the supplier's Terms tab and in supplier registration.
- Catalogue, product page, cart and agent prefer suppliers that deliver to the carpenter's region;
  a supplier that doesn't is marked "לא מגיע לאזור שלך" (or self-pickup only) and is not the
  default in `bestOffer()`.

## Notes
2026-10-08 (PR #16): migration `20261008130000_supplier_delivery` adds `suppliers.delivery_regions`,
`delivery_fee_excl_vat`, `free_delivery_from_excl_vat`. Regions are required at `/supplier/join`
and in the Terms tab. `lib/delivery.ts` reads them apart from the catalogue query (tolerates a
database without the migration). `loadProducts({ city })` sets `AppOffer.delivers` and skips a
non-delivering supplier as the suggested offer when another one delivers; product page and cart
show "לא מגיע לאזור שלך". Empty regions = unknown, shown as nothing.
Left: the agent chat (`lib/procurement-agent.ts` calls `loadProducts` without a city); showing
the delivery fee to carpenters; admin-created suppliers are not asked for regions. Migration not
yet applied anywhere.
