## Context
Carpenters and suppliers sign in with emailed links (`/carpenter/enter/[token]`, `/supplier/enter/[token]`), so whoever controls the inbox controls the account. That is the accepted model (same as a password reset), but today nothing tells the owner that a device was signed in.

## Done when
Opening a login link on a browser without a session cookie sends the account's email a short "נכנסו לחשבון ממכשיר חדש" notice with the time, for carpenters and suppliers.

## Notes
Owner asked about inbox takeover on 2026-10-05 and agreed the link model stays.
