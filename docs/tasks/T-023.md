## Context
Decided by the owner 2026-10-08 (plan doc "תוכנית: מפת הספקים והאדמין", section "מודל משולב"):
a combined model. Each supplier chooses "show prices" or "quote only". The owner's supplier
(איתמיר) keeps prices visible. Prices are never deleted; switching back is instant.

Today every live offer shows its price to a signed-in carpenter (`lib/app/catalog-server.ts`,
`lock_prices`). The closest thing to a quote request is "שאלה לספק" from the agent chat
(`supplier_contact_requests`, area 3), which carries text only and no price back.

## Done when
- A supplier setting (Terms tab) "להציג מחירים / רק הצעת מחיר".
- For a quote-only supplier, the server strips the price from catalogue, product page, search and
  agent results (not only hidden in the UI). Admin and other suppliers never see it either.
- The carpenter sees "בקש הצעת מחיר" instead of a price, can ask for a quantity, and the request
  sits apart from directly orderable lines in the cart.
- The supplier answers in the console (price, validity); the carpenter approves and it becomes a
  normal purchase order through `/api/orders` with the quoted price snapshotted.
- "בקש הצעה לכמות גדולה" is also offered on products that do show a price.
- The agent only prepares (add to cart / request a quote); the carpenter sends.
- Admin sees that a request exists and whether it was answered, not the quoted price.

## Notes
Marketing ("מחירי B2B אחרי הרשמה") gets "או הצעת מחיר מהספק" only once a quote-only supplier is live.

Built 2026-10-11 on the owner's word ("תבנה אותו בלי קשר לספק נוסף"), before a second supplier:
- Migration `20261011140000_price_quotes`: `suppliers.price_mode` ('shown' default / 'quote') and
  `quote_requests` (RLS on, no policy). Everything reads them tolerantly; without the migration
  every supplier shows prices and the quote screens say they open soon.
- Server: `lib/price-mode.ts` (who quotes only), `lib/quotes.ts` (client-safe types, states,
  labels), `lib/quotes-server.ts`, `lib/emails/quote.ts`. `loadProducts` sets `price: null` and
  `quoteOnly: true` on such offers and never lets their price decide the suggested offer; the agent
  does the same. `/api/orders` refuses a quote-only supplier's line without an answered quote,
  claims the quote before creating the order (no double use), snapshots the quoted price and
  stamps `quote_requests.order_id`.
- Carpenter: "בקש הצעת מחיר" on the product page (buy box and phone bar), lists, promoted card
  and reorder; "הזמנה גדולה? בקשו הצעת מחיר" on priced products; `/app/quotes` to add an answered
  quote to the cart or turn it down; quote lines in the cart keep price and quantity.
- Supplier: "מחירים באתר" switch at the top of the terms tab; "בקשות הצעת מחיר" at the top of the
  orders tab (price per base unit, valid until, note, or decline). One answer per request.
- Admin: suppliers list shows "רק הצעת מחיר" and request counts, never the price. A quote-only
  supplier that runs its own account has its prices hidden in `/admin/products` and kept out of
  the report averages.

Still open: the request does not open a message thread (T-025); campaign offer links
(`/o/[token]`, `lib/offer.ts`) still read the list price, though ordering from them is refused
for a quote-only supplier; nothing expires answered quotes except the date check on read.
Verified: tsc, build, lint at baseline, screens at 375px and 1280px from a preview page. Not run
against a database (no env files here).
