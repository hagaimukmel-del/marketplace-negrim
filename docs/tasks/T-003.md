## Context
Ad example: "הזמן לי לוח לבן וצירים, 20 יחידות, העדפה לספק X". `lib/procurement-agent.ts` parses intent by keywords and has no supplier-preference handling.

## Done when
That sentence (and variants) yields a cart with both items, quantity 20 rounded to pack/MOQ, and the preferred supplier's offer when one exists. Covered by smoke-test flow 9.
