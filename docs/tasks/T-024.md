## Context
Decided by the owner 2026-10-08: besides the subscription, a supplier will be able to buy one week
of promotion for 690 ₪ (excl. VAT; 95 ₪ since 2026-11-03). There is no place for it today. The closest mechanism is
`/admin/campaigns`, which already allows one active campaign at a time, and the roadmap shows an
introduction message beats a discount.

## Done when
- One "מוצר השבוע" slot per week, labelled as promoted, shown: top of the carpenter home (`/app`),
  top of the product's category in the catalogue, first suggestion with the label in the agent
  when the search is in that field, and in the weekly introduction message.
- Supplier console: a "פרסום" tab with a calendar of free weeks; the supplier picks week + product.
- Admin approves the week; admin sees all weeks and their results (views, add-to-cart, orders);
  each supplier sees the results of its own weeks.
- Payment stays outside the site, like the subscription (CLAUDE.md §5).

## Progress (2026-10-11, plan stage 7)
Built: `promo_weeks` + `promo_events` (migration `20261011130000_promo_weeks`), the supplier's
"מנוי ופרסום" tab (the subscription from `lib/supplier-plans.ts`, a calendar of the next 12
weeks, book / cancel, results of past weeks), `/admin/promotions` (approve, reject with a note,
withdraw a coming week, results), and the labelled card at the top of `/app` and of the
product's category, plus first place in the agent when the search lands in its main category
(`lib/promo.ts`, `components/app/PromotedCard.tsx`). Results: views and distinct carpentries,
adds to cart from the card, orders and amount for that product from that supplier during the
week (order_items, not cancelled).

Still open: the weekly introduction message (campaign) does not pick up the promoted product,
and adds to cart from the agent chat are not counted (its orders are). The booking stays a
request until approved; there is no payment state, billing is outside the site.

## Progress (2026-11-09): manual setting by the operator
At a supplier's request (owner), `/admin/promotions` can set a week by hand: pick supplier, a
product it sells live, a week (this week included, which a supplier cannot book, up to 12 ahead),
price on the card or not, the week's price (default `PROMO_WEEK_EXCL_VAT`, 0 for a free week) and
an internal note. It is saved approved at once and shows in the supplier's own tab. A taken week
answers 409; the operator can then replace it, which cancels the holder ("הוחלף ידנית באדמין")
and puts it back if the insert fails. Any booking that has not ended, the running week included,
can be edited: product of the same supplier, week, price on the card, week's price. Every change
is written to `admin_actions` (`set_promo_week`, `replace_promo_week`, `edit_promo_week`). No
migration: the card still takes its name, image and price from the product and offer.
