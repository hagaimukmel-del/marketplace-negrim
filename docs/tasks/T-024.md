## Context
Decided by the owner 2026-10-08: besides the subscription, a supplier will be able to buy one week
of promotion for 690 ₪ (excl. VAT). There is no place for it today. The closest mechanism is
`/admin/campaigns`, which already allows one active campaign at a time, and the roadmap shows an
introduction message beats a discount.

## Done when
- One "מוצר השבוע" slot per week, labelled as promoted, shown: top of the carpenter home (`/app`),
  top of the product's category in the catalogue, first suggestion with the label in the agent
  when the search is in that field, and in the weekly introduction message.
- Supplier console: a "פרסום" tab with a calendar of free weeks; the supplier picks week + product.
- Admin approves the week; admin sees all weeks and their results (views, add-to-cart, orders);
  each supplier sees the results of its own weeks.
- Payment stays outside the site, like the subscription (CLAUDE.md §5).
