## Context
Phase 1 of נגרים עובדים ביחד is in the code: board, posting, job page, my jobs, capabilities
profile, reports, `/admin/work`, and the founding subscription. It needs migration
`20261015130000_work_network` (four tables and the `work-images` bucket). Applied 2026-10-15 to
staging and, on the owner's yes, to production (Supabase connector; history rows set to the file's
version so `db push` stays in step). The board stays closed to carpenters until `WORK_BOARD_OPEN=true`; the operator
always sees it.

## Staging check, 2026-10-22
Done on staging (local server, `WORK_BOARD_OPEN=true` for the run) with two test carpentries:
profile, a job with a photo, the anonymous view from a carpentry without a profile and the name
after it fills one, a report reaching `/admin/work` with the counters, and closing the job from
`/app/work/mine` with an outcome. Not checked: the automatic pause after 3 reports, since only two
carpentries existed. A report stays open in `/admin/work` after its job is closed; the operator
closes it there.

## Board opened, 2026-10-25
The owner chose to open the board now instead of waiting for 30 profiles and 10 jobs:
`WORK_BOARD_OPEN=true` on Vercel production. Item 4 below is superseded by that decision.

## Done when
1. The migration is on staging (`npm run db:push:staging`) and the flow is checked there with
   `WORK_BOARD_OPEN=true`: fill a profile, post a job with a photo, see it on the board from a
   second carpentry without the poster's name, report it three times, restore it in `/admin/work`.
2. The migration is on production (explicit yes), and `npm run db:types` is committed.
3. Only then the PR merges. The pages read the new tables; before the migration they show an
   empty board, and saving a profile fails.
4. The board opens (`WORK_BOARD_OPEN=true` on Vercel) when there are 30 profiles and 10 open jobs,
   after phases 2 (conversations) and 3 (email notifications).
