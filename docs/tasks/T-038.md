## Context
A critical review of `/terms` (version 2026-11-03) on 2026-11-09 found gaps between the terms and the
site: no conditions on the supplier annual agreement, a supplier terms gate that showed carpenter points
and not the fee, a live contact address that is not the main one, no account deletion / retention text,
a privacy notice short of Amendment 13, and clauses a court may not enforce (liability cap of 0 ₪ for
carpenters, broad indemnity, exclusive Tel Aviv jurisdiction). No accessibility statement exists.

the version when merging.

## Owner decisions for the supplier annex (2026-11-09)
1. The 12 months run from the operator's approval of the supplier. Until then the supplier is on hold
   (except suppliers marked free).
2. No automatic renewal; a reminder 30 days before the end.
3. Leaving early only in special cases, agreed in a conversation between the supplier and the site.
4. A supplier the operator removes mid-term gets a pro-rata refund, unless removed for a breach.
5. Paid for the year up front, for 12 months, in 12 installments.
6. "מוצר השבוע" can be cancelled until the Thursday before its week.
7. איתמיר is a supplier unrelated to the platform. The operator is חגי מוקמל, עוסק מורשה 300009339;
   נגרים B2B uses that registration for now.
